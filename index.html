Case studies

AI delivery and product growth in practice.

01 Standard Data — Scaling an AI assistant for 3,500+ learners

From April 2025 to June 2026, I led the expansion of an educational AI assistant from a single course to 11+ courses, supporting 3,500+ learners across several major B2B clients. I defined the AI Assistant's product strategy, balancing answer quality, cost, latency, and scalability while prioritising access close to course materials to support retention within clients’ learning platforms. Over this period, course completion rose by approximately 20 percentage points, and instructor workload on learner-support tasks fell by approximately 90%.

02 Dr. Jay — Shortening the path to first value

As co-founder of an early-stage digital therapy product, I identified through user interviews and funnel analysis that a lengthy, detailed onboarding flow was causing users to abandon setup. I simplified onboarding to help users reach a first brief support session sooner, centred on an immediate concern and a practical two-minute exercise. Onboarding time fell by 30%, while day-14 retention increased by approximately two percentage points in a before-and-after comparison of new-user cohorts.

Dr. Jay was later acquired by Spectrum.Life, after my departure.

Co-founder · Product strategy & discovery

Selected advisory sessions

I advise businesses and their teams on AI integration, product decisions, and technical capabilities. These anonymised stories highlight the assessments, recommendations, and practical plans developed through that work.

01 Defining a measurable cross-selling pilot

A B2B distributor wanted product recommendations that would expand customers’ purchases, but needed a clear starting point and a way to measure value. I helped separate cross-selling from repeat-purchase use cases and recommended testing a simple purchase-pattern baseline before investing in more complex machine learning. The consultation produced an MVP and evaluation plan centred on new products purchased per customer, gross profit, and checks for cannibalisation.

Domain
B2B commerce
Advisory output
MVP scope and evaluation plan
02 Reassessing an underperforming AI vendor solution

A B2B distributor reported that its document-processing tool was falling short of expectations, with poor results discouraging staff feedback and limited visibility into the vendor’s system. Based on the client’s account, I helped assess the constraints and recommended reconsidering the supplier, alongside clearer requirements for data coverage, system access, and independent quality testing. The resulting recommendations gave the client a framework for evaluating a replacement and accepting future delivery, including part-time technical oversight.

Domain
B2B commerce
Advisory output
Supplier evaluation and acceptance criteria
03 Scoping an automation pilot for financial reporting

A real estate developer’s finance team spent several days assembling lender reports from inconsistent accounting and management data. I helped identify data consolidation as the prerequisite for useful AI automation and recommended a narrowly scoped reporting pilot, with human review for discrepancies. The consultation produced a staged plan: reconcile the source data, prototype one recurring report, and assess quality and speed before expanding.

Domain
Real estate development
Advisory output
Staged reporting automation plan
04 Testing the value of a complex AI experience

A founder exploring a conversational wellbeing product was considering a realistic video avatar and real-time emotion recognition, creating trade-offs around cost, complexity, and response speed. I helped challenge whether those components were necessary for the initial user experience and recommended testing a simpler voice-led prototype first. The consultation produced a validation sequence and suggested experience metrics to inform which capabilities merited further investment.

Domain
Digital wellbeing
Advisory output
Prototype priorities and validation sequence
05 Reviewing an AI specification before delivery

A real estate developer sought an independent review of a proposed AI system for matching construction-work descriptions to an internal catalogue. I helped identify gaps in model-selection evidence, validation criteria, automation thresholds, and maintenance responsibilities. The review produced concrete questions for the supplier and a clearer outline of the infrastructure and skills needed to evaluate and support the solution.

Domain
Real estate development
Advisory output
Specification review and supplier questions
06 Defining the first AI engineering hire

A business owner had built several AI-assisted tools but needed technical support to improve reliability and move beyond founder-led development. I helped translate the operational bottlenecks into a hiring profile covering backend development, AI integration, deployment, and code review. The consultation produced a draft role specification, a candidate-assessment approach, and phased pilot recommendations for document automation.
